## 🧠 What we’re building

We’re exploring a new foundation for AI compute: systems inspired by how the brain processes information, designed to make intelligence more efficient and bring it closer to the people and devices that need it.

## ✨ Our focus

| | |
|---|---|
| 🧠 **Brain-inspired architectures**<br>Rethinking how intelligence is represented and computed. | ⚡ **Efficient inference**<br>Exploring selective computation that can reduce unnecessary work. |
| 📍 **Edge-first AI**<br>Moving useful intelligence closer to devices and real-world applications. | 🛡️ **Sovereign infrastructure**<br>Building toward greater control over compute and data. |

## 🧭 Our direction

**EDGE** &nbsp; → &nbsp; **SOVEREIGN** &nbsp; → &nbsp; **UNIVERSAL**

Starting at the edge, we’re building toward sovereign enterprise systems and a broader cognitive compute layer.
