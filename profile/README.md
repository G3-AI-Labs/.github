## What we’re building

We’re exploring a new foundation for AI compute: systems inspired by how the brain processes information, designed to make intelligence more efficient and bring it closer to the people and devices that need it.

### Our focus

- **Brain-inspired architectures** — rethinking how intelligence is represented and computed.
- **Efficient inference** — exploring selective computation that can reduce unnecessary work.
- **Edge-first AI** — moving useful intelligence closer to devices, people, and real-world applications.
- **Sovereign infrastructure** — building toward AI systems that give organizations greater control over their compute and data.

## Our direction

We’re starting at the edge and building toward sovereign enterprise systems and a broader cognitive compute layer.
